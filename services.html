Frequently Asked Questions

Do you manufacture replacement parts?

No. Engineered Legacy provides reverse engineering services and documentation. Customers can use the completed files with their preferred manufacturing supplier.

Can you reverse engineer damaged parts?

Often, yes. The feasibility depends on the condition of the component and the amount of original geometry remaining.

What types of parts do you work with?

Industrial components, machine parts, brackets, housings, fixtures, and other mechanical components where original drawings are unavailable.

Need a legacy part documented?

Send us information about your component and we will help determine the next steps.
