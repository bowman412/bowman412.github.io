What We Do

Engineered Legacy helps manufacturers preserve critical engineering knowledge and reduce future downtime by reverse engineering spare parts into accurate CAD models and engineering drawings. Whether you’re preparing for the future by documenting parts while OEM support is still available or recreating documentation after it has ended, we provide the engineering data needed to maintain critical equipment.
